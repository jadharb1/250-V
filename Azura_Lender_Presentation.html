Non-Negotiables Our Walk-Away Terms — These Do Not Move
01

IFC credit committee requirement. Below 1.50x = no signature.
02
MIGA Political Risk Insurance — Mandatory
Full PRI for currency inconvertibility and transfer restriction. Est. 1% p.a. on insured exposure. Required by all commercial bank participants.

25% Minimum Equity Ratio
Sponsor equity at or above USD 225M at all times. Any restructuring reducing equity below this threshold is unacceptable to IFC credit committee.
IFC requires skin in the game. Lower equity = deal economics collapse.

Binding GSA from Ministry of Finance — not a letter of undertaking. Must cover WEC payment backstop, FX convertibility support, no discriminatory regulation.
Election late 2026. We need a contract, not a promise.
